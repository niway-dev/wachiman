# Wachiman trademark policy

Last updated: 2026-11-02

This policy says what you may do with the name and logo of Wachiman without asking, what needs
permission, and how to ask. It is short on purpose. If you are unsure, ask: you will not get in
trouble for asking.

In short:

- You can always say "Wachiman" truthfully: in articles, talks, documentation, "works with Wachiman".
- You can run it, configure it and redistribute unmodified copies.
- If you distribute a modified version, or its output reaches people outside your team, give it
  another name, remove the original logo and replace its Wachiman comment heading. You can say it is
  "based on Wachiman". If you change it only for your own team and nobody outside it sees the
  result, you may keep the name.
- Do not use the name or the logo as part of the name of a product, company, domain or service, or in
  a way that suggests we made or approved something.

## 2. What you can do without asking

Always, as long as you are accurate, do not suggest we made, approved or support your work, and use
the name no more than you need to:

- **Refer to Wachiman by name** in text, including criticism and comparisons.
- **Write and talk about it:** articles, books, videos, courses, podcasts, talks. You may show the
  logo, unmodified, to illustrate them. Screenshots of reviews that Wachiman posted are fine.
- **Say your work goes with it.** Put the name after a verb or a preposition and keep your own name
  for your own work: "works with Wachiman", "for Wachiman", "uses Wachiman", "compatible with
  Wachiman".
- **Run, configure and pin it.** Calling the reusable workflow, setting its inputs, adding your own
  review policy files and copying the files without changing them are all unmodified use. The
  reviews it posts say "Wachiman", which is correct.
- **Redistribute an unmodified copy,** exactly as published here, with the license and notices. It may
  keep the name and logo it came with.
- **Keep a fork on GitHub to prepare a contribution.** GitHub gives it the same name by default; that
  is a working copy. If you publish it with changes, section 4 applies.
- **Say that your work is "based on Wachiman"** or "a fork of Wachiman", in plain text, with a link
  to this repository.
- **Link to `https://wachiman.dev`** and use the name as the link text.
- **Describe a service you provide to someone who uses Wachiman,** such as "I help teams set up
  Wachiman". Do not call yourself official, certified, authorized or a partner.

## 3. What needs permission

Ask first (section 6) before you:

- Use the name or the logo as part of the name of a **product, service, app, plugin, bot, company,
  organization, project, event or user group**. "Wachiman Cloud" and "Wachiman Pro" are examples of
  names we would have to approve.
- Register or use the name in a **domain name, subdomain, social media handle, or an account or
  package name** on a registry or marketplace (for example npm, PyPI, container registries, GitHub
  organizations or the Actions Marketplace).
  - Exception: a repository or package name that begins with your own name and ends in
    `-for-wachiman` (for example `acme-reviews-for-wachiman`) is fine, as long as your product's
    display name and description follow section 2.
- Offer Wachiman, or something that looks like it, as a **hosted or managed service**, under any
  name that contains the mark.
- Put the name or the logo on **merchandise or printed goods,** sold or free.
- Use the logo as **your own branding:** as your project's, company's or bot's avatar, favicon, app
  icon, or main logo.
- **Change the logo** in any way.
- Say or suggest that we **made, approved, endorse, certify or partner with** you or your work.
- Use a name that is **confusingly close** to ours for a pull request review tool or a related
  service, such as a misspelling, a translation or a name that sounds the same.

If your use does not fit any list, treat it as needing permission and ask.

## 4. Forks and modified versions

The code license lets you change the code. When you do, you are the origin of that version. People
reading its output cannot tell it from ours unless it looks different, and our statements about how
Wachiman behaves, including what it can and cannot do with your key, were made about the version we
publish.

If you distribute a modified version, or what it publishes reaches anyone outside your own team
(comments on a public repository count), you must:

1. **Give it another name** that is not Wachiman and not confusingly close, including its repository,
   package, workflow title and documentation titles.
2. **Remove the logo:** the image files, the social preview image and every place that shows them. Do
   not use a mascot that looks like it.
3. **Replace the heading** `👮 **Wachiman**` that the software puts at the start of the comments it
   posts, and any other text that presents the output as ours.
4. **Say what it is, in words:** you may state that it is "based on Wachiman" or "a fork of
   Wachiman", with a link, and you must state that it is not affiliated with or endorsed by NIWAY.
5. **Keep the license notices** the code license requires. That is the license's rule, not this policy's.

You may keep the technical names that exist so setups keep working: the `/wachi` and `/wachiman`
commands, the hidden `<!-- wachi:… -->` markers in comments, the `AGENT_REVIEW_*` settings and the
`agent-review` file names. This includes legacy `agent-review`/`claude-review` state markers and
compatibility command aliases. They are interfaces, not branding.

If you change the code only for your own team and nobody outside it sees the result, you may leave
the name as it is.

If you have a small fix, such as a bug fix or a translation, and want to keep the name, ask first; the
simplest route is usually to send the change to us.

Renaming does not make a use allowed that the code license does not allow. The license decides whether
your use of the code is permitted at all; this policy decides what you may call it.

## 5. The logo

- Use the files as we publish them. Do not recolor, crop, stretch, rotate, outline or animate them,
  and do not combine them with other symbols, words or slogans.
- Leave clear space around it, and do not place it on a background that hides it.
- When you show it online, link it to `https://wachiman.dev` or to this repository.
- Do not use it as your own branding (section 3), and do not use it, or one that looks like it, to
  promote another tool.

## 6. How to ask

Write to **contact@niway.com**. Say what you want to do, where it will appear, and what it will be
called. This is a one-person project: we answer on a best-effort basis, and we cannot promise a
response time. A permission counts only if it is given to you in writing by NIWAY.

If you see a use that looks confusing or misleading, write to the same address. We would rather hear
about it than not.

## 7. If something goes wrong

Most problems are accidents. We will write to you first, say what needs to change and give you
reasonable time to change it. If a use is deliberately misleading, or a message is ignored, we may
ask the platform, registry or registrar that hosts it to act, and we may take other steps. Accurate
references, reviews and criticism are not what this policy is for.

Not acting on a use is not permission for it.
